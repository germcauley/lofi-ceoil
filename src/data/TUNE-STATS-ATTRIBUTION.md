# tune-stats.js

`src/data/tune-stats.js` is generated by `tools/derive-tune-stats.mjs` from
The Session's tune dump. It holds **distributions only** — how often a melodic
interval occurs, and which scale degree a part comes to rest on, per tune type.
No tune, setting, phrase or fragment of notation from the corpus is reproduced
in it, and the corpus itself is not redistributed here.

## Source

Data dumps of [thesession.org](https://thesession.org), published weekly at
<https://github.com/adactio/TheSession-data>.

Contains information from The Session, which is made available here under the
Open Database License (ODbL).

## Alterations

As required by the ODbL, this is what was done to the data:

1. Only the `type`, `meter`, `mode` and `abc` columns were read.
2. Each `abc` body was parsed into melody notes. Grace notes, chord symbols,
   text annotations, decorations, inline fields and repeat endings were
   discarded; a double stop was reduced to its first note.
3. Notes were converted to diatonic scale-step indices. Adjacent pairs within
   one part were counted as signed intervals; a rest ends a run.
4. The final note of each part was counted as a scale degree of that setting's
   own key, taken from the `mode` column.
5. Counts were normalised to proportions and rounded to five decimal places.

No row-level data survives step 5. To regenerate:

```bash
curl -sL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/adactio/TheSession-data/master/csv/tunes.csv -o tunes.csv
node tools/derive-tune-stats.mjs tunes.csv
```

you, <https://thesession.org> is worth supporting.
